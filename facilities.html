Parking Spots

Taman Tasik Putra provides a very large parking space so that visitors do not have to worry about parking their car outside. There are also parking spots in front of the food court. They're completely free of charge.

Resting Stops

If you're feeling exhausted after a long jog, or just want to sit back and enjoy the surrounding scenery, Taman Tasik Putra has a bunch of shelters scattered around the jogging tracks. Its concrete platform tiled with marble makes it strudy, and there's many room for everyone to wind down and build relationships.

Food Courts

Hungry? You can grab some food or a drink around the stalls scattered across the park area. The most famous stall is the laksa stall. There is also a food court where everyone can enjoy their food.

Restrooms

Taman Tasik Putra has some public restrooms that you can use. A small fee of RM 0.50 is required.

Playgrounds

Taman Tasik Putra has two playgrounds. Families who visit here can let their children have fun while they go for a jog, or sit on the shelters beside to relax.

Cycling and Jogging Tracks

Taman Tasik Putra has a large track surrounding the park area. Whether you want to jog, run, cycle or just take a walk, there's plenty of space for you.
