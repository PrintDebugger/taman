Healthy Lifestyle

Taman Tasik Putra provides accessible spaces for activites like jogging, cycling and other sports. This encourages regular exercise in a natural environment and combats health issues such as obesity and cardiovascular diseases.

A Place for Relaxation

Taman Tasik Putra consists of an environment that is filled with nature. From the lake to the greenery all around the park, people can spend their time to relax and enjoy the scenery. This helps us to wind down and reduce stress.

Social Interaction

Taman Tasik Putra serves as a gathering spot for families and friends, allowing them to strengthen their bond through recreational activites. There are also some occasional fitness events for everyone in the community to socialise and know each other.
